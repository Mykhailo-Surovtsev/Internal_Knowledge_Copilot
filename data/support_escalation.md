# Support Escalation Playbook

Escalate a support ticket to the urgent queue when a customer cannot access a core product function, reports a suspected security or privacy issue, or reports a duplicate payment or suspected fraud.

Before escalation, record the ticket ID, the customer impact, the affected product area, and the troubleshooting already performed. Notify the on-call engineer when the issue affects multiple customers or prevents a customer from completing a critical action.

After the incident is resolved, link the final incident or postmortem record to the support ticket so the team can recognize similar cases later.
